## Full description (Play 4000, Apple 4000)

```
GSU Alumni Connect is the official app of the Gombe State University Alumni Association. It keeps Gombe State University graduates connected to each other and to the University, wherever they are.

Every graduate already has a place. Your academic record comes from the University's own graduation records, so there is nothing to sign up for: activate your account with your registration number, confirm a detail from your record, and choose your own password.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Alumni directory: find classmates and fellow graduates by name, faculty, department, graduation year or state.
• Messages: talk to other alumni directly, in private conversations.
• Groups: you are automatically a member of your cohort, department, faculty and state groups. Post updates and join the conversation.
• Jobs: browse opportunities posted by fellow alumni, and apply with your CV.
• Events: see reunions, homecomings and Association events, and RSVP.
• Mentorship: offer to mentor, or find a mentor who walked the same path.
• Digital alumni ID card: a verified card with a QR code anyone can scan to check it is genuine.
• Alumni map: see how many graduates live in each state.

YOUR PRIVACY
• Your CGPA, degree class, JAMB number, date of birth and salary are never shown to other members.
• You choose whether to appear in the directory, and whether to show your email or phone.
• No ads, no tracking, and no selling of data. The app is free with no in-app purchases.
• You can delete your account from Settings at any time.

SECURITY
• New accounts are activated only after you confirm a detail from your University record.
• Repeated wrong attempts lock activation, and ID cards are issued only to confirmed members.

FIRST TIME?
Activate your account on www.gsualumni.org.ng/login, then sign in to the app with your new password.

Published by Gombe State University. Questions: alumni@gsu.edu.ng
```
