🔄 TWO-TIER PROCESSING
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Tier 1: INSTANT (Self-Trigger)
─────────────────────────────
✅ Fires immediately after job creation

Tier 2: SAFETY NET (Scheduled Cron)
───────────────────────────────────
